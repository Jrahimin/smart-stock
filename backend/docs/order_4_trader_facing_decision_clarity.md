## Reading order and compatibility

1. Show the **dated action** and its audience. For a prospective entry, put the current entry assessment and its specific reason beside or immediately below the action, ahead of bullish evidence in both visual and DOM order. For an existing holder, lead with the precise holder instruction and reason. Put fresh-entry context under “Adding to this position” only when relevant.
2. Show why this name deserves attention, the strongest available limitation, and what would change the assessment. On compact rows use one selection reason and one controlling limitation, with the rest on Detail. Use the overlay’s `reason_code` for the immediate explanation. Use `controlling_blocker` or the primary decision reason for distinct policy context when relevant. The evaluator copies the first published blocker even for other outcomes, so it must never override a later quote/path reason. Avoid repeating the same warning.
3. Keep a legacy finalized thesis and fixed levels when the action, meaning and date are identifiable. Unknown action or taxonomy gets an unavailable label. A quote, chart signal or missing decision cannot create WAIT, HOLD or a risk rating. UPDATING can carry the prior dated result. A date alone cannot establish current applicability. Show “Current entry unverified” for missing, incompatible, stale or mismatched current evidence. Distinguish this display fallback from the server having actually returned UNVERIFIED. On older clients keep existing fields and enum meanings. Deploy additive projections before their consumers and leave immutable publications intact.

## Increment 1 — Stock Details and the shared current-entry contract

**Required**

- Move the assessment from the chart-only presentation to the action block. Use all seven server states. For APPLICABLE say **“Quote meets the checked entry conditions as of [time].”** A BREAKOUT assessment requires a confirmed completed-session close and volume, followed by a permitted entry session. AWAITING_CONFIRMATION distinguishes the missing close/volume from waiting for that session. For PULLBACK, the evaluator checks the zone but does not measure how long support holds. Show “Quote is in the pullback zone; watch whether support holds” in that case. Use the plan’s dated condition for remaining qualitative guidance. Avoid “buy now,” promises of a fill, and a blanket request to reconfirm an already confirmed breakout. Owners: `entry_applicability.py:119–139`, `trade_plan.py:345,494`.
- Keep invalidation on the backend. `build_entry_path_facts` reads traded, valid, non-suspicious OHLC lows in the permitted sessions strictly after the actual Dhaka publication day. It recovers overwritten lows from retained current/previous revision payloads and excludes raw lows across incomparable price bases. A reliable provisional low can invalidate; only a completed finalized session can confirm a breakout. A later in-zone quote cannot revive that same plan after a supported stop breach. Missing/order-ambiguous evidence can yield PATH_INCOMPLETE; a known breach takes precedence over incomplete continuity. The repository’s earliest journal timestamp is table-wide, so it does not certify uninterrupted observation of every stock or tick. Describe a breach as **observed**. Use the evaluator’s result for late publications, withdrawals and basis changes. No browser breach history, synthetic stop event or new persistence. See `market_data_repository.py:174`, `market_universe_service.py:831`, `entry_applicability.py:96,143`, `test_entry_applicability.py:48,114`.
- Draw the preferred entry low–high band (ordinary bounds as fallback), independent marked quote, stop/invalidation and target range on one price scale. `trade-plan-panel.tsx:24` draws midpoint-to-quote and invents widths/markers for coincidence or absence; its model selects only the high target. Render equal bounds as a tick, omit missing markers, and keep numeric terms accessible. Show the supplied planned reward per unit of risk using the conservative lower target, upper entry and stop. Remove the UI color threshold at 1. Trailing plans with no fixed target get their management rule. Keep current status visible even without chart geometry. See `trade_plan.py:258–290`.
- Use existing summary, warning, constraint and component codes first. `trader-decision-reason.ts:273–340` parses English sentences for numeric reason parameters; Detail independently selects trend/momentum signals. Where a required localized sentence actually uses a parsed value, read its typed field or expose that specific missing parameter on the existing projection. Reuse the summary’s selected component/constraint in Detail if needed to displace its conflicting selection. For legacy rows use a translated code-level explanation without unsupported numbers. Keep full free-text source detail secondary. No general explanation schema or new ranking.

**Refresh boundary.** Retain the browser fixes: no-store SSR, generation-keyed workspace/universe queries, Pulse seed/briefing alignment, IndexedDB TTL/schema validation and coordinator invalidation on quote, decision **or status-only** changes.
