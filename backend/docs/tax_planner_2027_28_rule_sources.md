# Tax planner AY 2027–28 source verification

Reviewed 5 November 2026. This pack projects liability on supported facts for the income period 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2027. The following checks distinguish an assessment year expressly named in law, amendments that continue from their first applicable year, and approvals tied to a payment or disposal date.

References to the Finance Act, Paripatra and supplied Nirdeshika below use one-based PDF pages. The rule pack identifies the exact editions by SHA-256 and separately identifies the official 177-page press edition of the Nirdeshika.

| Rule family | Evidence for AY 2027–28 |
| --- | --- |
| Thresholds, slabs and individual minimum | Finance Act 2026, Act 96, §160(1) and Schedule 2 Part 1 paragraph (a), PDF 142–143, expressly name AY 2027–28 and AY 2026–27. The applicable table starts at 400,000 taka and uses 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% bands. The accompanying conditions govern special profiles, the disabled dependent addition, and the 5,000 ordinary and 1,000 eligible new taxpayer floors. The separate later-year schedules start at AY 2028–29. [Finance Act §160](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59534.html) · [Finance Act PDF](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/Finance_Act_2026.pdf) |
| Employment and ordinary income | Sixth Schedule Part 1 paragraph 27 grants the one-third exemption. Act 89 §135 increased its cap to 500,000. Act 96 §157 adds paragraph 37 for group insurance benefits without replacing paragraph 27. Paripatra Appendix 9 PDF 267 identifies AY 2026–27 as the first assessment year for the 2026 Sixth Schedule amendment. The engine uses the continuing provision for qualifying private employment and accepts documented ordinary income amounts in the precomputed routes. [Act 89 §135](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1718/section-59129.html) · [Act 96 §157](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59531.html) |
| Investment rebate | Income Tax Act §78 as consolidated by the Ministry includes the amended 3% income limb, 10% investment limb and 750,000 taka ceiling, with the exclusions from the income base. Act 96 §61 changes the latter two limits with effect from 1 July 2026. Paripatra PDF 263 identifies AY 2026–27 as the first applicable assessment year. The provision has no expiry restricting it to that year. [Income Tax Act §78](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51908.html) |
| Eligible payments and investment caps | Income Tax Act Sixth Schedule Part 3 retains the 120,000 DPS and 500,000 government securities limits and the conditions for insurance, annuity, PF, pension, superannuation and welfare payments. See the English Gazette PDF 273–275 and recognized PF conditions at PDF 241–243. Act 89 §135 requires new purchases of exchange-listed securities. Act 96 §157 retains the existing DPS and government security caps, changes the specified fund category from the 500,000 cap to new purchases, and adds the holding and early recovery condition. This condition is effective from 1 July 2026 and matters during the forward income period. Prior rebate recovery requires review. [English Gazette](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/Income_tax_act_2023.pdf) · [Act 89 §135](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1718/section-59129.html) · [Act 96 §157](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59531.html) |
| Approved recipients and CZM | Sixth Schedule Part 3 paragraph 2(11) supplies the continuing statutory zakat fund category. SRO 213/2026 adds the named recipients from 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2030. Their approvals intersect the entire forward income period and are evaluated against the payment date. Unknown approvals need review. CZM is a separate Part 2 bank-transfer deduction under Act 96 §157 and falls under §77(2), including the 10% aggregate pre-donation income condition. The planner verifies supported CZM-only claims within the limit and requests review when the claim exceeds it or other Part 2 claims may share the limit. [SRO 213/2026](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/sros/SRO-213,_08_Jun,_2026.pdf) · [Income Tax Act §77](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51907.html) · [Act 96 §157](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59531.html) |
| Financial income and dividend | Section 62 governs financial asset income. Act 96 §158 replaces Seventh Schedule paragraph 2 and specifies 15% for natural-person dividends. Paripatra PDF 267 identifies AY 2026–27 as the first year of the Seventh Schedule amendment. Act 96 §160(3) separately dates the changed withholding and advance-tax rules to the income year starting 1 July 2026. The planner treats ordinary interest or profit as gross income and preserves actual tax paid separately from dividend liability. [Act 96 §158](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59532.html) · [Act 96 §160](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59534.html) |
| Listed gains | Act 89 §135 and Sixth Schedule Part 1 paragraph 36 supply the annual 5,000,000 exemption and qualifying-disposal restrictions. Act 89 §136 re-enacts Seventh Schedule paragraph 1(b) at 15% for securities of exchange-listed companies. Act 96 §158 leaves that clause and paragraph 1(c)'s holding-period treatment in force. Earlier SRO 383/2024 was repealed by SRO 151/2025, recorded in the 2025–26 Paripatra, so the current rate rests on the replacement statute. Fund units can qualify for the exemption without establishing the rate on an excess. That rate requires confirmation whenever aggregate qualifying gains cross the shared exemption. [Act 89 §135](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1718/section-59129.html) · [Act 89 §136](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1718/section-59130.html) · [Act 96 §158](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59532.html) · [2025–26 Paripatra](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/news-scroller/Income-tax_Paripatra_2025-2026.pdf) |
| Turnover, special regimes and wealth | Section 163(6) covers gross business receipts. SRO 324/2026 changes the ordinary category in table row 4 to zero through 20,000,000 taka, with no sunset in the notification. See Paripatra PDF 235 and 267. The engine uses it only to screen ordinary businesses below the ceiling. Sectored or special businesses, carryforwards, foreign income, complex losses, vehicle taxes and surcharge triggers remain review cases. Act 96 §160(5) expressly includes AY 2027–28 for its separate surcharge schedules. [Income Tax Act §163](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51993.html) · [Paripatra](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/news-scroller/Aykor_Poripothro_2026-2027_BG_Press_Modify_Copy.pdf) · [Act 96 §160](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/section-59534.html) |
| Credits and settlement | Paripatra PDF 264 gives AY 2026–27 as the first assessment year for amended §§163, 170 and 173. Section 163 treats supported documented payments as eligible credits subject to its exceptions. The forward pack keeps annual liability and credit information separate and leaves settlement unavailable. Filing-window adjustments, extensions and interactions require independent confirmation before the product supports a forward-year payable estimate. [Income Tax Act §163](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51993.html) · [Income Tax Act §170](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52000.html) |
