The ranking gate requires 60 mature dates, 200 evaluable selections, ≥0.25 percentage-point net excess improvement, ≥10% relative precision improvement, a positive lower paired-block bound and safeguards. The entry gate counts **distinct challenger dates with accepted, available filled outcomes**, requires 200 such outcomes in each arm, positive cash-inclusive gain and lower bound, acceptable cohort losses and no worse drawdown (`backtesting_promotion.py:321–389`). At five picks per date, 200/300 = 66.7% per arm even with full slates. Research selects the top five eligible names; canonical execution separately requires permission. Three zero-buy dates in six publications illustrate why a 60-date entry estimate is optimistic. The existing gate uses a frozen test partition capped at 60 dates by default. If the 60-date window cannot reach 200, merely collecting more dates while always truncating to 60 cannot accumulate the missing fills. Predeclare a cumulative forward cohort or longer fixed assessment window **before recording selections**, keeping all thresholds, empty dates and reporting cadence fixed. Keep retrospective splits as configured. Review readiness weekly; perform the formal comparison at the predeclared endpoint, avoiding repeated efficacy tests.
